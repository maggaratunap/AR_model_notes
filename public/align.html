Control points 0

Turn on Add point and click sharp, easy-to-find corners at floor level: column base corners, wall corners, door jambs, floor drains. Pick 3 or 4 points spread far apart (ideally 10 m+). Each point saves a photo of the view to help you find it on site.

    Printed markers (Android, optional)

    Tape printed markers on columns or walls at chest height. After one good point alignment on an Android phone, look at each marker for 2 seconds and the phone learns where it is. From then on anyone can just point a phone at a marker to align.
